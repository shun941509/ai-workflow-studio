# Fictional Cedar Desk — confirmed retry boundary

Source ID: EN-RETRY-71. Rule ID: CD-R71. Original fictional note; no real external operations occur.

A retry is a new attempt after a confirmed not-started result or an explicit reconciled failure. Keep the logical operation ID when the operation contract requires it. Do not treat CD-R71 as CD-R17: retrying an operation and resuming a review are different actions.

An unknown outcome requires reconciliation. This note contains no automatic retry interval, no guarantee of delivery, and no authorization to bypass a pending review.
